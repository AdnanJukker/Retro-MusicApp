## Brand & Style

This design system channels the tactile precision, mechanical romance, and typographic discipline of late 20th-century audio engineering. Drawing from mid-century Braun industrial design, Sony's golden-era portable electronics, and meticulously typeset vinyl gatefold sleeves, the identity treats recorded music as a physical artifact rather than transient data streams.

The interface combines retro-tactile precision with modern, whisper-quiet editorial minimalism. Physicality is expressed not through gaudy skeuomorphic bevels, but through intentional structural constraints: rigid mechanical grids, crisp hairline chassis separations, physical toggle states (rocker switches, mechanical push latches), and instrument-grade readouts (VU needles, mechanical tape counters, frequency scales).

The emotional tone balances warm, analog nostalgia with clinical acoustic integrity. It evokes the quiet focus of dropping a stylus into a lead-in groove, inspecting liner notes on textured uncoated cardstock, and adjusting weighted aluminum potentiometers in a dimly lit listening room.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unbleached Japanese cotton paper, brushed chassis metal, amber status lamps, and archival lithography inks.

- **Primary Background (`#F3E9D2` - Warm Cream):** The base canvas tone. Evokes aged heavy-weight vinyl sleeves and off-white audio hardware housings. All primary views sit directly on this canvas.
- **Secondary Background (`#D9C7A3` - Muted Beige):** Applied to inset chassis sections, cassette wells, recessed control panels, and active media docks to create structural architectural division without harsh elevation.
- **Primary Dark / Text (`#24201C` - Deep Espresso Brown):** Replaces pure digital black entirely. Provides deep contrast with organic warmth for typography, micro-borders, and structural frameworks.
- **Secondary Text (`#6C6259` - Patina Charcoal):** Used for metadata, secondary technical readouts, run-times, and non-active controls, ensuring strict typographic hierarchy.
- **Accent (`#C65D32` - Burnt Orange):** The primary signal color. Reserved for active play states, recording indicators, active needle peaks, and tape heads.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D6A84B` - Mustard Yellow):** Used for auxiliary state highlights, stereo balance center notches, tape type indicators (Type I / CrO2 / Metal), and curated badges.
- **Supporting (`#6D7055` - Muted Olive):** Applied to calibration indicators, equalization curves, tape side markers (SIDE A / SIDE B), and subtle system tags.
- **Hairline Dividing Line (`rgba(36, 32, 28, 0.15)`):** Strict 1px borders used throughout the system to section interface bays, mimic machine plate joins, and establish structural grids.

## Typography

The typography strategy unites three distinct operational eras:

1. **Barlow Condensed (Display & Titles):** Emulates classic LP album packaging and silk-screened receiver chassis panels. Headlines render primarily in uppercase or tight title case with controlled tracking, lending physical mass to artist names and track groupings.
2. **Manrope (Body & Narrative):** Provides contemporary legibility and effortless reading rhythm across track listings, liner notes, and long-form artist histories without conflicting with the condensed display scale.
3. **Space Mono (Technical Telemetry & Hardware Labels):** Operates as instrument markings. Used exclusively for machine settings, sampling frequencies (`96.0 KHZ / 24 BIT`), format flags (`STEREO`, `DOLBY NR`, `SIDE A`), counter numbers (`04:28:12`), and playback speeds (`33⅓ RPM`). Space Mono is strictly uppercase for indicators and telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors modular rack-mount audio component architecture (DIN rail and 19-inch rack units). The screen is treated as an engineered deck divided into precise functional modular bays.

- **Grid Strategy:** A structural 12-column grid on desktop and tablet; a 4-column system on mobile. Each zone (decks, visualizer, track queue, specs bar) snaps cleanly into hard-bordered containment lines.
- **Chassis Sectioning:** Instead of floating whitespace, functional areas are divided by full-bleed or framed `1px solid rgba(36, 32, 28, 0.15)` rules.
- **Rhythm & Insets:** Internal component padding operates on an uncompromising 4px/8px modular base. Elements inside audio equipment decks hug tight mechanical margins (`space-sm` and `space-md`), balancing breathing room with the high-density utility of precision electronics.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: Single-column rack stack. The primary deck or cassette visualizer anchors the top, physical transport controls sit in a locked bottom shelf, and library content scrolls in between.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1024px)`: 8-column layout. Cassette/vinyl visualization occupies a dedicated upper quadrant; dual tracklists and mechanical EQ meters rest side-by-side below.
  - `Desktop (> 1024px)`: 12-column layout. Split studio architecture: fixed Left Bay (4 columns) houses transport, real-time VU telemetry, and tape transport status; Right Bay (8 columns) delivers the vinyl sleeve editorial catalog, liner essays, and cue lists.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects ambient dropped shadows and modern diffused floating blur surfaces. Visual depth is realized entirely through physical inset, mechanical recession, and plate layering:

- **Surface Tiers & Insets:**
  - *Base Chassis:* `#F3E9D2` (warm flat ground).
  - *Recessed Bays & Cassette Wells:* Rendered in `#D9C7A3` with a 1px solid `#24201C/15` border. Inner drop shadows are constrained to `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(36, 32, 28, 0.08)` to simulate the interior depth of a stamped metal or molded plastic well.
- **Crisp Structural Borders:** Every interface segment, audio deck plate, and interactive unit is bounded by crisp hairline strokes (`rgba(36, 32, 28, 0.15)`). Active modules step up to `rgba(36, 32, 28, 0.6)`.
- **Mechanical Tactility (Engaged States):** Physical rocker switches, latch buttons, and key decks feature a subtle 1px bottom edge lip (`#24201C`) when resting, which translates down (`transform: translateY(1px)`) with the elimination of the lip when pressed, producing an immediate mechanical snap.
- **Micro-Texture Overlays:** A fine 2% analog grain filter sits statically over the viewport canvas to evoke unbleached paper grain and brushed anodized faceplates, completely suppressing digital sterile flatness.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is uncompromisingly sharp: `roundedness: 0`. 

- **Corners:** Buttons, cassette bay windows, album sleeves, telemetry badges, and structural containers feature pure 0px right angles. This preserves the authentic industrial silhouette of 1970s audio monitors, amplifier housings, and vinyl record jackets.
- **Exceptions for Mechanical Functionalism:** Circular geometry is permitted solely where real-world hardware mandates it: rotary volume knobs, tape reel hubs, vinyl disc profiles, and round status LEDs. These circles live inside strictly squared rectangular frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Transport Controls
- **Mechanical Latch Buttons (Play, Pause, Stop, Cue):** Rectangular buttons with a 1px solid `#24201C` border. Background is `#F3E9D2` at rest. Labeling uses `label-caps`. When active or playing, the button fills with `#24201C` and shifts text to `#F3E9D2`, with an optional Burnt Orange (`#C65D32`) pip illuminating in the corner.
- **Rocker Switches (Power, Dolby, Mono/Stereo):** Two-state dual-block toggles. Divided by a center seam; the active side displays `#24201C` text with a sunken `#D9C7A3` background, while the inactive side is muted `#6C6259`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Technical Audio Badges:** Crisp bordered rectangles with zero border-radius. Monospaced font (`Space Mono`, 10px bold, tracking `0.12em`). Examples: `[ 24-BIT / 96KHZ ]`, `[ STEREO ]`, `[ TYPE II CrO2 ]`. Inactive badges have subtle outlines (`#24201C/20`); active signal badges invert to `#6D7055` or `#C65D32` fills with `#F3E9D2` text.

### Lists & Track Table
- **Archival Cue Sheet List:** Styled like an engineering studio tape box cue sheet. Monospace track numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`) in `#6C6259`, followed by the song title in `Manrope 14px Medium` (`#24201C`), and runtime in `Space Mono 12px`.
- Rows are delineated by thin hairline lines (`1px solid rgba(36, 32, 28, 0.12)`).
- Hovering or highlighting a track activates a light `#D9C7A3` tint across the row and exposes an inline mechanical play prompt.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Mechanical Push Latches:** Radio buttons are represented as square checkboxes with sharp edges. When selected, they display a solid inner square block (`#24201C`) centered with a 2px margin, rather than a checkmark or rounded dot.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Indexing Inputs:** Recessed fields with `#D9C7A3` fill and a 1px border of `#24201C/30`. Typography uses `Space Mono 13px`. Placeholders sit in `#6C6259` with a persistent typewriter cursor block (`#C65D32`) on focus.

### Cards & Sleeves
- **Vinyl Gatefold Cards:** Clean, square-cut containers (`border-radius: 0px`). Album artwork displays completely unrounded with a 1px hairline border around the sleeve perimeter. Below the sleeve, metadata is typeset with `Barlow Condensed` for album title and `Space Mono` for year and catalog number (`CAT. 1974-A`).

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Dual Analog VU Meter:** Dual rectangular meter displays with warm cream backgrounds, stamped decibel graduation lines (`-20` to `+3dB`), and an animated analog needle pivoting from a lower center anchor. Needle changes color from `#24201C` to `#C65D32` beyond the 0dB overload threshold.
- **Mechanical Tape Counter:** 4-digit mechanical rolling wheel container with an inset shadow (`#D9C7A3` background, `#24201C` digits in `Space Mono`), complete with a dedicated 12px square reset button.
