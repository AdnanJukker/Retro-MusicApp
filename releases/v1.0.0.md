# Hi-Fi Archive 1.0.0

The first Android release of Hi-Fi Archive, a retro-inspired music player.

## Included

- Music discovery and search using the JioSaavn catalog.
- A full player and mini player with seeking, queue controls, shuffle, and repeat.
- Liked songs, recent plays, followed artists, and preferences saved on your device.
- Lyrics when available from the music source.
- Playback and appearance settings, including queue continuation, history, artwork motion, and compact track rows.
- Preservation of existing saved YouTube track IDs.

## Install

1. Download `Hi-Fi-Archive-v1.0.0-android.apk` from **Assets** below.
2. Open it on your Android device and allow installation from the browser or file manager if prompted.
3. Launch **Hi-Fi Archive**. Expo Go and a development server are not required.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the APK checksum. The automatically generated source archives are for developers.

## Playback notes

- Listening requires an internet connection; liked songs are not offline downloads.
- Search results and lyrics depend on the upstream music service. The backend may take time to wake after inactivity.
- Previously saved YouTube tracks depend on the YouTube resolver and may be unavailable. Search again to choose a version from the current catalog.
