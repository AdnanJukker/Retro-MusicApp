# RetroWave 1.0.1

The app formerly known as Hi-Fi Archive is now **RetroWave** — same retro
hi-fi music player, new name and app icon.

## Included

- Renamed the app from Hi-Fi Archive to RetroWave, with a new vinyl-and-tonearm
  app icon, launch screen, and Android adaptive icon set.
- Lock screen and notification playback controls now include seek
  forward/backward buttons, not just play/pause.
- Everything from 1.0.0: JioSaavn-backed search, full player and mini player
  with queue controls, shuffle, and repeat, liked songs, recent plays,
  followed artists, preferences saved on your device, and lyrics when
  available from the music source.

## Install

1. Download `RetroWave-v1.0.1-android.apk` from **Assets** below.
2. Open it on your Android device and allow installation from the browser or file manager if prompted.
3. Launch **RetroWave**. Expo Go and a development server are not required.

If you have Hi-Fi Archive v1.0.0 installed, installing this APK updates it in
place — your liked songs, history, and preferences are kept.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the APK checksum. The automatically generated source archives are for developers.

## Playback notes

- Listening requires an internet connection; liked songs are not offline downloads.
- Search results and lyrics depend on the upstream music service. The backend may take time to wake after inactivity.
- Previously saved YouTube tracks depend on the YouTube resolver and may be unavailable. Search again to choose a version from the current catalog.
